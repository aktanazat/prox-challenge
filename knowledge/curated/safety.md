## Fume & Gas Safety [owner-manual p.3]
Welding/plasma-cutting fumes are an inhalation hazard linked to cancers (larynx, lung), early-onset Parkinson's, heart disease, ulcers, reproductive organ damage, intestinal/stomach inflammation, kidney damage, and respiratory disease. Use natural/forced ventilation and a NIOSH-approved respirator. Do not use near degreasing/painting operations. Keep head out of fumes. Use exhaust-at-arc ventilation. Confined spaces require good ventilation or an air-supplied respirator. Have an Industrial Hygiene specialist check air quality per OSHA PEL/ACGIH TLV guidelines.

## Arc Ray Safety [owner-manual p.3]
Arc rays injure eyes and burn skin. Wear ANSI-approved eye protection with **minimum shade 10 lens**. Wear leather leggings, fire-resistant shoes/boots; avoid cuffed pants/open pockets/catch-prone clothing. Keep clothing free of grease/oil/solvents. Wear approved head covering, apron/cape/sleeves/shoulder covers/bibs. Wear an approved welding jacket/long sleeves. Use flame-resistant ear protection when welding overhead/confined spaces.

## Electrical Safety [owner-manual p.4]
Electric shock can kill. Turn off/disconnect/discharge electrode to ground before setting down torch/holder or servicing. Don't touch energized parts; wear dry insulating gloves. Connect only to a grounded, GFCI-protected supply. Don't use near water/damp objects. Pacemaker wearers must consult a physician before use. Don't expose to rain/wet conditions. Don't abuse the cord (no carrying/pulling by cord; keep clear of heat/oil/sharp edges/moving parts). **Do not use outdoors.** Insulate yourself from the workpiece/ground with dry insulating material. Must be connected per applicable electrical codes.

## Fire Safety [owner-manual p.4]
Arc and slag can cause fire. Clear/protect flammables in a **35 ft (10 m) radius** around the work area; cover openings with fire-resistant material. Keep an ABC fire extinguisher on hand. Maintain a clear, well-lit work area. Don't operate in atmospheres with reactive/flammable liquids/gases/vapors/dust. When working on a metal wall/ceiling, protect the far side from ignition or post a fire watch (during cutting + at least 30 min after). Don't weld/cut materials with combustible coatings/structure without an approved hazard-elimination method. Don't dispose of hot slag near combustibles. Inspect thoroughly for fire evidence after welding (delayed smoke/flame is possible). Clean/purge/vent containers that held unknown or combustible substances before applying heat.

## Welder Use & Care / Maintenance [owner-manual p.5]
Don't use if the switch doesn't control power on/off. Unplug before adjustments/accessory changes/storage. Prevent unintentional starting (switch off before connecting power or moving the welder). Store out of reach of children; only trained users should operate it. Use only as instructed for its intended operations. Do not use for pipe thawing. Inspect for misalignment/binding/breakage before use; repair before use. Service only by a qualified repair person with identical replacement parts. Maintain labels/nameplates (request replacements from Harbor Freight if unreadable/missing). Unplug before any inspection/maintenance/cleaning.

## Gas Cylinder Safety [owner-manual p.5]
Cylinders can explode when damaged. Never weld on a pressurized/closed cylinder. Never let the electrode holder/electrode/torch/wire touch the cylinder. Keep cylinders clear of electrical circuits. Keep the protective cap on except during use. Use gas equipment designed specifically for the process and maintain it properly. Protect cylinders from heat, impact, slag, flames, sparks, arcs. Use proper procedures to move cylinders.

## Grounding [owner-manual p.6]
Incorrect grounding wire connection can cause electric shock/death. Have a qualified electrician verify outlet grounding if in doubt. Don't use the welder with a damaged cord/plug. Never use adapter plugs — have a proper outlet installed instead. The cord's green wire connects only to the welder's grounding system, never to a live terminal; never leave it disconnected or modify the plug.

## Extension cords
**Do not use an extension cord with this welder.** Use only the supplied power cords or an identical replacement; don't install a thinner/longer cord; don't patch cords together (risk of moisture ingress → shock). [owner-manual p.6]

## Process-specific safety callouts
- **MIG/Flux-Cored setup**: "PARTS MAY BE AT WELDING VOLTAGE" — keep hands away from the Wire Feed mechanism; close the door before plugging in (unless cold-feeding); don't touch the Trigger while feeding wire through. [owner-manual p.16]
- **TIG**: Do not weld without the Grounding Clamp attached; when not holding the torch, rest it on a nonconductive/nonflammable surface; only hold the TIG Rod with an insulated welding glove; metal work bench must be grounded; shielding gas can displace air and cause asphyxiation — do not open gas without ventilation and fix leaks immediately (gas without CO2 is especially hazardous since asphyxiation can start without shortness-of-breath warning). [owner-manual p.30]
- **Stick**: Do not weld without the Grounding Clamp attached; when not holding the Electrode Holder, rest it on a nonconductive/nonflammable surface. [owner-manual p.32]
- **Tungsten grinding**: wear a respirator + ANSI-approved safety goggles (some electrode additives are hazardous to breathe); dedicate a fine-grit wheel exclusively to electrode grinding to avoid contamination. [owner-manual p.26]
- **All welding**: minimum shade-10 full face shield/welding mask, ear protection, welding gloves, sleeves and apron, NIOSH-approved respirator, fire-resistant clothing without pockets. [owner-manual p.18, p.28]
- **Vehicle work**: disconnect the vehicle battery (both terminals) before welding on/near a vehicle, to protect its electronics from high-voltage/high-frequency welding bursts. [owner-manual p.19]
- **Maintenance/service**: unplug the welder and let it cool on a heat-proof, non-conductive surface before any service. [owner-manual p.41]
