### R5 — Anonymous, June 6, 2024
**Title:** "Gas leak. Poor weld penatration/. Ultimately not worth the m[oney]"
**Verified buyer:** VERIFIED BUYER
**Helpful:** 9 up / 1 down

> I purchase this to upgrade from my chicago electric mig 170 that worked alright but wantes to upgrade to a mult-proces machine. I had high hopes for this machine but unfortunately it must have bought one built on a Friday before a long weekend. First I bought a vulcan tig torch and the lead would not fit in the power connection. Second I could not get it to lay a nice bead. Then when mig welding with gas it had weak peneteation and fusion that. I had to grind out the entire weld joint. Third it had an internal gas leak. Ended up losing almost a half bottle of argon before I caught it. I ultimately returned everything and went to HD and picked up an AHP Dual proceas tig stick machine. The Vulcan 220 Omnipro only came with a 90 day warranty and a 2 year warranty for an addition $300. The AHP welder from HD came with a 3 year factory warranty and a 3 extented warranty for $100. The price of machine is what attracted me to it but after buying the warranty, Tig torch and foot pedal. Then having nothing bi It issue in just the first week it truly is not worth the money. Ultimately I would not recommend this machine.
