## Multi Process Machine – Vulcan Omni Pro 220 – Any good for beginner?

- Source: https://www.weldingweb.com/threads/702243
- Started by nikb, Sep 11, 2018 · 31 posts

**OP [nikb]:** Just to be clear, I will be welding at home for a hobby... Currently I have a Hobart handler 140 running 20oz CO2 tanks... The multi process machine seems an interesting option as it would allow me to try it all with one machine. I would like to add a spool gun to the mig [to] do light aluminium work, and use tig for various things, including stainless and aluminium if possible. Is this a good option, and is the Vulcan a good machine?
> **[nikb]**, same day: OK, from looking more closely, looks like aluminium TIG is out, as this is only DC, but thats not that big of a deal to me.

**[Bonzoo]:** Ask again in 30 years. One thing about the Vulcans is I hear you can play with it a[nd] take it back if you think it sucks... I'm an old fart who believes one machine one process.......one lifetime.

**[smithdoor]:** The Vulcan line is new to market. Just [a] little bit more you can buy a name brand [—] I would like Miller, Hobart or Lincoln. As the spoolgun goes I did all my Aluminum welding with 10FT MIG gun and a Teflon liner.

**[shovelon]:** Gotta tell you that the alum spool gun is a far cry from an AC tig welder. And switching back and forth from mig to tig on a multiprocess machine is for me a deal breaker... I have 15 welders and none are multiprocess. They all serve a function they are best at. And I would never buy a welder with plasma mode.

**[nikb]:** ...Looks like if i used this to do all types of welding, i would need 2 gas bottles. 75/25 mix for MIG and 100 argon for TIG. However, would i use the 100 argon for the spool gun as well?
> **[Bistineau]** To weld aluminum, YES.

**[Louie1961]:** I don't think there is anything wrong with the multi process approach for hobby work. **The vulcan is basically a copy of the Lincoln 210MP.** If you go to welding tips and tricks, Jody has a bunch of videos of him or JD welding all sorts of things with the 210MP. I say try the vulcan for a year and if you don't like it you really aren't out any money. Just don't buy the spool gun at first. Buy the basic unit and get to know it.

**[Iain P]:** I just bought an Omni Pro to add to my welder collection, I can quit any time I want. As far as I can tell the tig with pedal mode goes like this: turn on gas at torch, touch tungsten to workpiece, press pedal, finally lift tungsten and the arc will start. The arc stops once you let off the pedal and you have to turn the gas off manually. **I consider it and the Lincoln 210mp as 2.5 welders in 1. It's a mig, stick, and half a tig welder in one box.**

**[Louie1961]:** I had an omnipro for a short while. I loved the way it MIG welded, but the unit I had would not stick weld at all. I ended up taking it back. I believe it was damaged in transit.

**[danielplace]:** No hi frequency on that machine. Lift start. It isn't so bad... I think you would be fine with lift start if it were a dc only machine.

**On the parts-availability worry (recurring theme across this corpus):**
> **[cmh31909]** ...does anyone know how you would get it repaired or if HF would sell you replacement circuit boards or other parts if you needed them? That would be my main concern with buying one....what happens outside of the warranty/exchange period.
> **[shovelon]** Well this is just a theory. Harbor Freight has over 900 stores... I would assume [most of the parts are] the same ones Lincoln uses in their version that Harbor Freight patterned the Omni. I would bet if you pulled a working board out of an Omni, take a trip to China, you could find a shop to clone as well.
> **[Broccoli1]** Doubtful they will sell parts as their repair policy requires you to bring the item to an authorized repair facility or ship it to them. [quotes the service-plan fine print: "4. IF YOU NEED SERVICE: Please call 1-888-838-3421..."]

**Owner field reports once nikb was leaning Vulcan:**
> **[CactusWelder]** Had my OmletPro for 6 months now. Absolutely love the MIG and settings are real close. Been learning to TIG and lift arc is no big deal if you got the pedal... I got the spool gun but haven't tried yet. To use it you plug it's gas line to [the] Port on front of machine. That means you have to connect Argon to [the] rear Port. **This was a stupid thing HF did. They could have added another Port on rear so Argon could stay connected as it's used for TIG and spool.** ...Overall it has been a great welder for a great price.
> **[Iain P]** The preset wire feed speed seems just a touch too fast but I've always found factory wire speed recomendations to be a little fast regardless of brand. I think they err on the side of fast to keep from burning up tips. [running .030 ER70S-6 on 240V]

I think the OMNI is a pretty good fit for me. Will take a bit to gather the money, but i should have one by winter." No follow-up purchase report from nikb appears in this thread.
