## Vulcan OmniPro 220 Parts? — sourcing DINSE cables and connectors outside HF

- Source: https://www.weldingweb.com/threads/vulcan-omnipro-220-parts.701865/
- Started by acolic, Aug 17, 2018 · 18 posts, spanning 2018–2024

**OP [acolic]:** ...I saw an open box Vulcan 220 multiprocessor welder [at Harbor Freight]... ended up getting it for $250 [open-box, 7-day warranty]... while it starts I am missing three parts which after calling HF they are going to try [and] see if they are lying around but I doubt it. I need the 220V power cord, stick/electrode holder and the ground cable. I tried surfing the web but I can't seem to find an online parts dealer that deals with Vulcan parts. Any ideas for replacement parts?

**[henry42]:** The connectors should all be available as they are one of a number of industry standard parts. Just go look at the display model and you'll be able to match them up.

**[Louie1961]:** The power cable can be made with standard parts from Home Depot. It's a standard twist lock plug on one end and a 6/50 plug on the other. **The electrode holder and ground leads are standard DINSE connectors, they don't need to be Vulcan brand, just the correct size DINSE.**
> **[Sberry], 5 years later:** Louie bud,,, I think the power cord is special for these. For giggles I looked but they dont list squat.

**[shovelon]:** My guess is that they used the same style parts as the linclone [Lincoln] counterpart. So if they are available from Lincoln they should fit. [Links usaweld.com and eastwood.com DINSE ground cables/electrode holders as likely fits.]

**[acolic]:** I checked the mig assembly and it says I need a DINSE 35-70... The mig gun's connector says DINSE 35-70 but a number of reviewers say that the connector is a DINSE 50. They are not the same correct?
> **[soutthpaw]:** The number relates to the Maximum cable diameter it can accept. Only the dinse 25 is different. Which is 3/8 plug 35, 50, 70 are all 1/2" plug. Soo all are the same 35 and up.
> **[Louie1961]:** There really are only two common sizes in use. I no longer own the Omnipro 220, but if memory serves me correctly it is the larger size [i.e. 35/50/70, not 25].

**Resolution — acolic:** "Since it['s] was an As Is sale Harbor Freight won't replace any of the missing components. I ended up ordering a power cord from them for $15 but declined ordering the ground cable and the electrode cable. Each was $40 and I figured I could make them each a lot cheaper... **It took a bit of time to find the correct connectors. I went to two welding part stores and they couldn't match the connector. I took a chance on Amazon and found the right connector and made a ground and stick cable.** For future reference in case someone else finds themselves in the same spot the right connector is: amazon.com/gp/aw/d/B011MLJ06U. In retrospect I should have bought a red connector for the stick cable."

**Later confirmations (2023–2024):**
> **[taz911]:** My omipro 220 is just a few months old and the dinse connector has 35-70 on the connector end. [confirms the connector size is consistent across production years]
> **[SidecarFlip]:** I put the HF 3 year replacement guarantee on mine. If it pukes within 3 years or less, HF will give me a NEW machine. Not a bad guarantee though it does cost extra... Just added an Everlast cooler to my Pro-Tig as well as a flex head CK water cooled torch (series 2) and the DINSE connectors on the Pro-Tig are 35mm. Had to buy a rather expensive 35mm gas-through CK Dinse connector... Not overly fond of the stock HF torch actually. It's kind of stiff and the rigid head makes it worse. Went with 25 foot leads and extended my ground clam[p] to 25 feet as well but I upped the cable size to 4 gauge.
> **[ME JACKSON], 2024:** I need a circuit board from omnipro220 — [no reply; thread dies unanswered on the one part category (control boards) that isn't a generic DINSE/twist-lock commodity part]

**Takeaway:** cables/torch/ground connectors on the OmniPro 220 use industry-standard DINSE 35/50/70 (all the same physical 1/2" plug size; only DINSE 25 differs) and a standard twist-lock + 6/50 power cord — all sourceable generically (Amazon, welding supply, even Home Depot for the power cord) rather than through Harbor Freight/Vulcan-branded parts. The one unresolved category, confirmed by a dead-end 2024 post, is internal electronics — no source for a replacement control board ever surfaced.
