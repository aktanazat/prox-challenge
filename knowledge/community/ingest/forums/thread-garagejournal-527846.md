## Vulcan Omnipro 220 Welder — clearance-rack buy advice

- Source: https://www.garagejournal.com/forum/threads/vulcan-omnipro-220-welder.527846/
- Started by PearlWhiteGT, Jan 22, 2024 · 11 posts

**OP [PearlWhiteGT]:** Any of you guys have experience with the Vulcan Omnipro 220 Welder? My local HF has one on the clearance rack for half price.

**[Mgdoug3]:** I like to look at the one star reviews when looking at something from Harbor Freight. You have to select 1 star review because HF only show 5 star reviews by default. **The reoccurring theme with the 1 star reviews is that once the machine quits, you scrap it because no parts are available to fix it.** If you decided it's worth the gamble, get the 2 year warranty and hope for the best. If it's on sale, there's a good chance it's being dropped or replaced with a newer model. I'm not sure how the 2 year replacement warranty will work if it's no longer available. Me personally, I would rather buy a different machine that can be repaired and not a throwaway unit.

**[driftpin]:** What is 'half-price?" Half of $1150, or $1100? ... You might want to check the paperwork as there's a coupon in there for an accessory. The box doesn't come with a spool gun, and I think no TIG torch, so one more reason to check what's there in the returns/open box table... **Download the instruction manual before you return to the store and take inventory of what comes with the box, so you can check things off as being there (or not).** ...Take a look and see what spool is on the machine, I bet it's the flux-core wire for 'no-gas' welding. See how much wire is still on the spool, and compare that to a spool of the same weight/size on the shelf, to determine how much use it got before being returned. I let an acquaintance [a professional welder] use it to do some welding for a car project... He went to work, and worked continuously, until two motor mount blocks were done, and the brackets for the transmission mount was, too. He never had to stop because something tripped (we had it wired to a clothes dryer 240V outlet). The machine never shut-down because the duty cycle was exceeded. At the end of the work, he said, "I would use this box, any time!"
> **[PearlWhiteGT]**, replying with the actual price: $571.99 to be exact. I'm considering going back and opening up the box to check on all the things.

**[dnschmidt]:** I like HTP but a lot of people like the Vulcan welders. Parts availability always concerns me and that's my issue with Vulcan. If I were considering a welder from China like the Vulcan I would go with Primeweld. Their customer service is out of this world.
> **[dnschmidt]**, later in-thread: For that price if it blows up in five years who cares? It always comes down to value for money. If it's cheap enough and does the job parts availability down the road is less of an issue.

**[Wamsutta]:** You're kidding right? They actually named it the 220? Not only did they copy the cabinet style, they had to copy the name too. [referring to the Lincoln 210MP lineage]

**[emeraldcoupe]:** not the omipro 220, but i've had a migmax 215 since 2018, not a single issue with it.

**Resolution / field report — [Shoreline_]:** "**I've had mine since 2018 and the wire feeder just stopped working recently. It's ok I use it as a stick welder anyways.**" [The same failure mode — trigger-activated feed dies while the machine otherwise still runs — shows up independently in the Reddit ingest corpus, thread-1g5zlae.]

**[pancholasvegas]:** Not the OmniPro, but I rolled the dice on a HF ProTig 205 on an open box sale... One of the best purchases that I've made. I wouldn't have any hesitation grabbing this one if I was in the market, especially for open box pricing.

**Takeaway:** this is the corpus's clearest "how to evaluate a clearance/open-box unit" checklist — verify price against the coupon/accessory paperwork, download the manual and inventory the box contents against it before leaving the store, check the flux-core spool's remaining wire as a proxy for prior use, and specifically test it on a real, continuous job (not just a test bead) to confirm it doesn't fault on duty cycle. Parts availability is the single most repeated worry in the whole thread — echoed independently by three different posters (Mgdoug3, dnschmidt, and this forum's cross-reference in report.md's u/Fabrik8 NC4x4 quote) — but is treated by half the thread as a dealbreaker and by the other half as irrelevant given the price.
