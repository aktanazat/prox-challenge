## Longevity / reliability

> "Bought mine in 2019 with a 20% coupon I found online..... ZERO issues, love the thing the only caution its pre programmed settings tend to be too hot gotta back the amps down and run a few test beads great machine tho"
— @paul-j3j6f, [7 Year Update](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iggJs9-rAuQ)

> "I have one I've used professionally for two years, burned up 15lbs of wire in 8hrs, been thrown down a few staircases and slammed around in the truck. Looks like it's been through hell and back but still lays down a good bead."
— @ignite_welding, [7 Year Update](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iggJs9-rAuQ) — also pushes back on the video's "7 years, barely used" durability claim: "looking at the case on the welder and the gloves after 7 years this machine has been very very lightly used. Can barely get a set of gloves to last me a month let alone 7 years."

> "I LOVE mine! I've had mine just as long. A clone of a Lincoln... I settled with this and have ZERO REGRETS."
— @Joeak74, [7 Year Update](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iggJs9-rAuQ) — same comment flags a Harbor Freight parking-lot sale bringing the OmniPro 220 down to **$850** (Aug 12–18, stackable with the store card for another 10% off).

> "Ive had mine for about 3 years now. I run 0.30 to 0.35 wire setup with 220 and gas. Its been a great welder. I've had 0 issues with it. After getting your heat and speed set right she's been pretty solid on every job I've used it on."
— @lifeisgood3589, [2 Years Pros and Cons](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NRnkrWOkaqY) — also reports a colleague who runs one professionally at a steel-mill maintenance contractor and "loves it."

> "I bought a used Vulcan 220 OmniPro from a pawn shop, almost new condition..missing argon regulator. Thought the worst, but brought it home and tested it on flux core and stick 7018 and to my amazement it welded grrat so im going to keep it!"
— @richardsmalley122, [2 Years Pros and Cons](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NRnkrWOkaqY)

## Shop-floor comparison vs. name brands

> "We have been running an Omnipro 220 in a small manufacturing plant right alongside Millermatic 252s for almost 2 years. We replace liners about a third more often than the Millers and go through about the same amount of other consumables. When I sold the company and retired I purchased a new Omnipro 220 for my home shop where i make steel tables, work on my Jeep and pick up the occasional fab job. More than enough for a home fab shop"
— @robertworzalla2204, [2 Years Pros and Cons](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NRnkrWOkaqY)
