## Settings mentioned
- Only tested SMAW (stick) and flux-core arc welding; explicitly has not tried GTAW/TIG or GMAW/gas-MIG [01:30], [05:00]
- **7018 1/8" stick rod** and **.035" innershield flux core** both "turned out pretty well" fabricating a metal bench [03:30]
- DC-only machine (DC+ and DC-), no AC output [04:00]
- Modes with inductance, hot start, and arc-force adjustment available on stick/flux ("some of those more fancy options that you'd see on a little bit higher-end machines") [04:00]–[04:30]
- Drive roll accepts both **10 lb and 2 lb spools**; ships with a **.035" and .045" drive roll**; single-groove (not dual) roller [06:30]–[07:00]
- Included contact tips are **.030" only** — .035/.040/.045 must be bought separately [03:00]

## Quirks / failures reported
- **Power switch failure mode flagged by community, not this reviewer directly**: "some people have mentioned that the switch can get stuck in the middle between the on and off position and it can damage the internal electronics of this machine — be wary of that." [05:30] — corroborates the fried-switch failure documented elsewhere in the community corpus (u/Shrimpkin, r/Welding).
- MIG gun build quality is budget-grade: plastic handle/trigger/nozzle, thin metal hook, unthreaded pull-off nozzle [02:30]–[03:00]
- Electrode holder and work clamp are "sturdier" than expected but noticeably smaller/cheaper than Lincoln/Miller equivalents [02:00]
- Only a **90-day limited warranty** from Vulcan; reviewer recommends buying the 2-year extended warranty [08:00]

## Mods
